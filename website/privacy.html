SK Note Taker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

SK Note Taker ("the app") is a native macOS application that records meetings, transcribes them, and generates summaries. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled. The app is operated by the SK Note Taker developer, contactable at email@saqibkamran.com.

The short version

  • Your recordings, transcripts, and summaries stay on your Mac.
  • We do not run a server that receives your meeting content or your Google data.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google Calendar data

If you choose to connect your Google account, the app requests read-only access to your Google Calendar using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly scope.

What we access: the details of upcoming events on your primary calendar, such as event title, start and end time, location, and any meeting link.

How we use it: solely to display your upcoming meetings on the app's home screen so you can start taking notes for them. That is the only use.

What we do not do: the app never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events. It does not read past events beyond the upcoming window it displays. It does not transmit your calendar data to any server operated by us or to any third party.

Where it is stored: your Google sign-in tokens are stored only in your Mac's system Keychain. Event details are held in memory to render the upcoming list and are not written to a remote database.

SK Note Taker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Meeting recordings and transcripts

Audio you record, the transcripts produced from it, and the AI summaries are stored locally on your Mac. Transcription and speaker separation run on your device. AI summaries are generated through the Claude Code command-line tool installed on your own machine.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your Mac, you control it. You can delete any recording from within the app, and you can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the app's Settings, which removes the stored sign-in tokens from your Keychain. You can also revoke the app's access from your Google Account permissions page.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, calendar data, or meeting content with any third party. No human at SK Note Taker reads your Google data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
