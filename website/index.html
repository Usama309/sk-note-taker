Google Calendar integration

When you connect your Google account, SK Note Taker requests read-only access to your calendar (the calendar.readonly scope). It uses this for one purpose: to show your upcoming meetings on the home screen so you can start taking notes with a single click.

SK Note Taker never edits, creates, or deletes calendar events. It does not sell or share your calendar data, and it does not send that data to any server we operate. Your sign-in tokens are stored only in your Mac's Keychain. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Private by design

Recordings, transcripts, and summaries are stored locally on your Mac. Transcription and speaker separation run on your device. The app is open source, so you can read exactly what it does.
